How to count nodes

  1. Pick 3 plants that look typical for the field.
  2. On each plant, count the nodes on the main stem. A node is where a leaf joins the stem.
  3. Add the three counts, divide by 3, and enter the average.

Good to know

  • Coordinates are needed because temperature and day length change with location.

About the model

Limits
  • Trained on soybean trials from Arkansas, Minnesota, Ohio, Virginia and Wisconsin in 2017 and 2018. It has not been validated in Nigeria.
  • It predicts five stage groups, not the individual stages.
  • It does not use weather, which also affects how fast soybean develops.
  • Treat results as an estimate and confirm with the field signs in the guide.
